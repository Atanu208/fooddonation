About FoodShare

FoodShare is a two-sided marketplace that connects donors who have surplus food with NGOs and charities that can get it to people in need.

The Problem We Solve

Food Waste

Hotels, restaurants, bakeries, and households throw away tonnes of edible food every single day.

Hunger

Millions of people in India go to bed hungry every night despite the abundance of food around them.

No Connection

Donors don't know which NGOs are nearby and reliable, and NGOs lack a real-time feed of surplus food.

Our Solution

For Donors

  • Post surplus food with a few clicks
  • Reach verified NGOs in your area
  • Track pickup & delivery in real time
  • Generate tax-exemption and CSR reports

For NGOs

  • Live feed of available donations by city
  • One-click claim & pickup scheduling
  • Status updates keep donors informed
  • Impact analytics for reporting
